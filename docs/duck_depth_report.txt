Duck depth: why the deeper duck was not applied
================================================

Request
-------
Make Max duck lower: bend her legs more, bend forward at the waist and lower her head.

What happened
-------------
The duck pose (swingkit/moves/noncombat.py, DUCK) was changed step by step and rendered after each
change. Any of these changes makes a flat, pale lavender patch appear behind her back, just left of
her head, on the ducking frames:

  1. Hips lower than 7 px (tested 8, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14 px), even with the head level.
  2. Waist bend above 16 degrees (tested 20, 24, 26, 28 and 30), even at the original 7 px hip drop.
  3. A head offset that lowers the head against the hips (tested head=(1, 3)).

The original duck (hips 7 px down, bend 16, lean 2, head level) is the deepest combination that
renders cleanly, so it was kept. The rig checks (python -m swingkit) pass for all tested poses; the
patch is a drawing problem the checks do not look for.

Probable cause (not yet verified in the code line by line)
-----------------------------------------------------------
swingkit/rig.py, draw_hair_pass():

    _paste(C, UNDER, UNDER >= 0, *skirt)      # underpaint rides with the skirt/waist
    _hair(..., top_off=head, bot_off=skirt, ...)

The underpaint (UNDER) is a solid fill in the hair colour behind the head and beside the torso. It
is pasted at the skirt offset, while the long hair that normally covers it is stretched between the
head offset (which includes the waist bend and lean) and the skirt offset. When the head moves far
from the skirt (a deep bend, a lowered head) or the pose gets deeper, the hair no longer covers the
top of the underpaint, and the uncovered part shows as the flat patch.

Suggested fix (needs approval: it changes a shared renderer used by every animation)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Paste the upper part of the underpaint (the rows behind the head) at the head offset instead of the
skirt offset, or clip the underpaint to the area the hair covers. Every animation would then need
to be rebuilt and re-checked (python -m swingkit), and the GIFs in docs/ regenerated, because the
same renderer draws all of them.

Online search
-------------
This is custom rig code in this repository, so there is no outside documentation for it. No fix was
found online.

Update
------
The user chose to keep the full kneeling duck and accept the patch. DUCK now drops the hips 16 px
with the rear heel up, bends 24 degrees at the waist and lowers the head 3 px; the patch shows and
is left alone.
