Pad

Keyboard: arrows = pad, Z = A, X = B, C = X, V = Y, Q = L, W = R. A gamepad works too, wired or Bluetooth, including on an Android phone in Chrome (A = bottom face button, B = right, X = left, Y = top, L/R = shoulders or triggers, d-pad or left stick). Click the view first if keys do nothing.

Enemies walk in from the right; any attack that touches one kills it. Press H to show hurtboxes (yellow) and hit shapes (red).
